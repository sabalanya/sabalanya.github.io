Sergio Álvarez Balanyá

Applied Scientist · Amazon · Madrid, Spain

I am an Applied Scientist at Amazon and a researcher with a PhD from the Autonomous University of Madrid. My work centers on probabilistic machine learning, including calibration, uncertainty quantification, and Bayesian methods, with applications spanning speech, vision, and language models. I am currently interested in generative models and their application to action prediction and policy learning.

  • Projects

    • Probabilistic Fusion and Calibration of Neural Speaker Diarization Models

      Code for combining outputs from multiple speaker diarization systems using probabilistic fusion and calibration techniques such as Dynamic Logits Fusion and logistic calibration to improve prediction reliability.

    • N-Way-Attention

      Explores a generalization of the standard attention mechanism that attends over n-tuples of tokens simultaneously, implementing and evaluating a 3-way attention variant that sums over trios of tokens.

    • Adaptive Temperature Scaling

      Implements adaptive temperature scaling for neural network calibration, replacing a single global temperature with a lightweight per-sample function of the logits to better handle varying uncertainty across easy, hard, and near-OOD samples.

    • Machine Learning in Numpy

      Collection of Numpy implementations of some traditional ML algorithms. Visual examples are provided in the form of jupyter notebooks.
